# About Me

I like figuring out how things work and making them easier for the next person. When I’m new to a problem, I read what I can, talk to people who know the details, and pull what I learn into a clear explanation. I especially enjoy helping someone through a process for the first time. I help them see what matters, give them a place to start, and stay with them until the job is done.

That’s what I enjoyed about building the Intel Labs Compliance Portal. Researchers had to piece together guidance from different places to release their software. I brought the policies, tools, and documentation together so teams could see what applied to their project and what to do next.

Outside work, I’ve followed my curiosity into LEGO robotics and, more recently, watercolor. Watercolor is well outside my comfort zone, which is part of why I’m proud I’ve stuck with it. It’s teaching me to slow down, be patient with myself, and make time for something I’m learning just because I want to.
