    Security Researcher and Product Security Expert

    Research Operations, Intel Labs
    2019–Present

    • Led onboarding, live training, and hands-on support for Intel’s GitHub migration across hundreds of repositories.

    • Served as acting technical lead during team turnover, mentoring engineers and delivering releases for disability-access initiatives.
    • Coordinated with Intel’s Product Security Incident Response Team (PSIRT) to resolve a critical security vulnerability.
